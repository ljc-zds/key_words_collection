# SeaImagine homepage observations

- Source: https://seaimagine.com/
- Captured: 2026-09-24
- Method: rendered DOM inspection in the in-app browser

## Structure

- One creation platform is positioned around AI art, image, and video.
- The rendered homepage exposed 75 unique internal paths: 16 model paths, 13 blog paths, 7 `free-*` paths, 15 localized paths, and 24 other paths.
- Page families include generic video/image tools, model pages, scenario tools, free tools, blog articles, pricing, and language versions.
- Representative scenario pages include lip sync, birthday video, cartoon video, motion control, avatar, old photo restoration, and photo-to-cartoon.

## Shared commercial layer

- Shared credits, one-time pricing packs, image/video generation, premium models, generation history, queue priority, privacy controls, and commercial licensing.

SeaImagine uses fewer visible entry pages than Fylia but follows the same taxonomy and monetization pattern.
