## Structure

- The homepage focuses the brand on fashion, try-on, creators, and e-commerce while retaining a general image/video generation workspace.
- The rendered homepage exposed 102 unique internal paths: 31 model paths, 8 `free-*` paths, 15 localized paths, one visible blog path, and 47 other paths.
- Page groups include Video AI, Image AI, Try On AI, Free Tools, models, articles, pricing, and application downloads.
- Scenario pages include clothes, tattoos, hair, glasses, jewelry, product video, UGC, influencers, TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, profile pictures, and fashion models.

## Shared commercial layer

- The same workspace switches between video, image, and virtual try-on modes.
- Shared credits, pricing, history, private generations, commercial rights, and model selection support every keyword page.

The site changes the brand and default use case, but still exposes broad image, video, model, and free-tool coverage.
