# Fylia homepage observations

- Source: https://fylia.ai/
- Captured: 2026-09-24
- Method: rendered DOM inspection in the in-app browser

## Structure

- One shared creation workspace covers video, image, and music generation.
- Navigation groups pages under Video AI, Video Effects, Image AI, and Free Tools.
- The rendered homepage exposed 150 unique internal paths: 50 model paths, 13 blog paths, 7 `free-*` paths, 15 localized paths, and 65 other paths.
- Footer links expose many more keyword pages than the primary header.

## Page families observed

- Generic tools: `/ai-video-generator/`, `/ai-image-generator/`, `/image-to-video/`, `/ai-text-to-video/`.
- Model pages: `/model/{model-slug}/` for Flux, Kling, Seedance, Vidu, Wan, Veo, Sora, and other models.
- Scenario tools: avatar, poster, headshot, Ghibli, action figure, realism, music video, old-photo animation, and similar pages.
- Free acquisition tools: Image to Prompt, Image Describer, Background Remover, image conversion, upscaling, and free model pages.
- Blog detail pages support model releases, comparisons, prompt guides, and use cases.
- The footer links to 15 language-prefix versions.

## Shared commercial layer

- Shared credits, pricing, generation history, private generations, commercial rights, and queue priority.
- The free tier includes limited generations and 15-day history; paid credit packs extend history and unlock premium generation.
